TECHNOVA SOLUTIONS — IT SECURITY POLICY
Demo dataset | Version 1.0 | Effective 1 January 2026

1. Passwords and Authentication
- Use a unique password for every company account.
- Do not share passwords with colleagues, managers, or support staff.
- Use the company-approved password manager to store work credentials.
- Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) wherever it is supported.
- Report suspected account compromise to the IT Help Desk immediately.

2. Devices and Software
- Company-managed devices must use approved endpoint protection and receive security updates.
- Do not install unapproved software or disable security controls.
- Lock your screen whenever you leave your workstation.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT as soon as possible.

3. Email and Phishing
- Do not open unexpected attachments or sign in through suspicious links.
- Verify unusual payment, password-reset, or data requests through a separate trusted channel.
- Forward suspected phishing messages to security@technova.example.

4. Data Handling
- Store company files only in approved company storage locations.
- Share confidential files only with authorized recipients and use access-controlled links.
- Do not upload confidential company or customer data to personal cloud accounts or public AI tools.

5. Incident Reporting
- Report suspected malware, data exposure, unauthorized access, or accidental disclosure to the IT Help Desk immediately.
- Include the approximate time, affected device or account, and a short description. Do not attempt to investigate beyond your authorization.

6. Support
- IT Help Desk hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
- Contact: it-helpdesk@technova.example
